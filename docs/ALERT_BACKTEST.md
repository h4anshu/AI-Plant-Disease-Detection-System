# Alert rules replayed on past weather

Generated by `npm run alerts-backtest` on 2026-10-27. **Read the method first:** the weather that really happened (Open-Meteo archive, ERA5 reanalysis, 0.25 degrees) stands in for the forecast, so this is a *perfect forecast*. It shows how often each rule speaks and how many alerts a farmer would get; it says nothing about how good a real forecast is (real ones are worse, especially for rain and for the third day ahead). Local extremes are smoothed by ERA5. Places are city centres and the crop stage is held fixed through the season. Only the weather rules are replayed (not the official warnings, the river, fire or disease reports). The thresholds are still drafts: this is a check of noise, not a validation.

An "alert" is one rule on one date (a frost that shows 2 days ahead, then 1 day, then tonight is one alert). A "tip" is the lowest level; only watches and warnings are counted in the "per week" column that is compared with the target of at most 2 notifications per field per week.
